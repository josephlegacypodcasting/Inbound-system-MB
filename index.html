  2. People click a CTA.
  3. They land on a page.
  4. They download a resource.
  5. GHL captures the lead.

  8. If they're not ready, they stay in nurture.

  10. When the system works, we save it as a Snapshot — then clone it for every new client.

06 — Forms & Landing Pages

The first point of conversion

Keep the form short — more fields reduce conversion. Landing pages are built externally (Claude, Codex, Webflow, Framer, WordPress, or custom code) and connected to a GHL form, so GHL stays the backend CRM and automation engine without limiting design flexibility.

Form fields

Required: First Name, Email, Company Name
Optional: Website, Podcast Name

On submission, GHL automatically…

Creates/updates the contact → tags source & lead magnet → updates custom fields → creates an opportunity → moves it to "Lead Magnet Downloaded" → sends Email 1 → notifies the internal team → starts the nurture workflow.

09 — Reporting

Knowing what's actually working

The dashboard should answer real attribution questions, not just display vanity metrics.

Key metrics tracked

Leads by source · landing page conversion · form submission rate · email open/click rate · CTA click rate · discovery calls booked & completed · no-show rate · proposal sent rate · close rate · revenue · time-to-call · time-to-close.

Attribution questions to answer

Which channel generates the most leads? Which lead magnet converts best? Which emails drive the most CTA clicks? Which source creates the most booked calls and the most customers? Where are leads dropping off?
